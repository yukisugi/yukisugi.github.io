## 英語版（Russo lab原文、参考として保持）

### Opportunities

We are always on the lookout for enthusiastic new colleagues to join the group and welcome informal enquiries. More information can be found below.

### Graduate students

PhD projects are available in the lab, leading to a PhD in Natural Sciences from the University of Cambridge. More information on the LMB PhD programme is found here. Masters students are also welcome to get in touch regarding potential projects or rotations in the lab.

### Postdoctoral fellows and associates

Potential postdoctoral candidates are encouraged to contact Chris to discuss possible positions in the lab. An email including a cover letter which describes your previous experience and future goals, and your up to date CV with personal references is requested. We are happy to support potential fellowship applications; more about the postdoctoral program at the MRC-LMB can be found here.

### Visiting scholars and summer students

Undergraduate students from Cambridge and other universities interested in working in the lab on a summer research project or lab experience rotation are welcome to contact Chris about potential projects. Please email with a letter describing your prior experience (research, practical, computational or other), your relevant coursework, and an estimate of how much time you plan to spend in lab. We are also happy to host other scientists for short visits to the lab to work on specific projects. Please email to discuss potential collaborations.
